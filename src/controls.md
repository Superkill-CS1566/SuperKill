# Controls for the demo (Management Week 2) 

### Player 1 (left)
`WASD` movement, Press `G` to attack,
Press, `T` to throw/pickup
### Player 2 (right)
`Arrow key` movement, Press `H` to attack,
Press, `Y` to throw/pickup
### Debug controls
Press `1` to kill p1, 
Press `2` to kill p2
Press `f1` to see weapon hitboxes
