# Controls for the demo (Management Week 2) 

### Player 1 (left)
`WASD` movement, Press `G` to attack,
Press, `T` to throw/pickup, Press `B` to block

### Player 2 (right)
`Arrow key` movement, Press `H` to attack,
Press, `Y` to throw/pickup, Press `N` to block

### Debug controls
Press `1` to kill p1, 
Press `2` to kill p2
Press `f1` to see weapon hitboxes

### Autoscroll Rules
Each player gets three lives, when one dies, the level changs scroll direction
The player must then run to their side of the level once three lives are up
